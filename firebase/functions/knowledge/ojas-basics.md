## What Ojas Healing is
Ojas Healing is the spiritual community of Swami Anand Swaroop (called Swamiji). Swamiji guides the Ojas Healing family through meditation, self-healing and spiritual knowledge. His teachings are on the Ojas Healing YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@OjasHealing

## What the Ojas Family app is
Ojas Family is the community app of Ojas Healing. In the app, members read the Thought of the Day, follow guided practices (Blue Healing Light, Quick Protection, Morning and Night), ask questions, share their daily seva and grow together on the spiritual path with Swamiji.

## Topics Swamiji teaches on the channel
The Ojas Healing channel has playlists on: Questions and Answers by Anand Swaroop; Maharshi Patanjali's Yoga Sutras; the Self Healing Course; the Shiva Sutras of Vasugupta; Chakras; Body Chakras and Kundalini; All about Meditation; Vigyan Bhairav Tantra (Hindi); the Spiritual Encyclopedia (Adhyatmik Gyan Kosh); Protection Vidhi (Spiritual Protection Meditation); and spiritual videos in Hindi and English.

## The guided practices in the app
The Practice tab has four guided practices, one meditation each:
1. Blue Healing Light: the Blue Light Meditation for healing.
2. Quick Protection: Protection Vidhi 1, the protection shield of divine energy (Spiritual Protection Meditation).
3. Morning practice: the Higher Self meditation.
4. Night practice: the Night Meditation.
Finishing a practice (listening to almost all of it) earns Moksha Points once a day for each practice. Morning and night practices open at Level 4 or with membership.

## Protection Vidhi series
Protection Vidhi is Swamiji's series of spiritual protection meditations on the channel: Vidhi 1, the protection shield of divine energy; Vidhi 2, spreading divine energy to any place; Vidhi 3, increasing spiritual energy; Vidhi 4, cutting the negative energy of thoughts (negative energy removal); Vidhi 5, the night meditation. There is also a video on how to protect your home.

## Practice streak
The Practice tab shows a streak: the number of days in a row on which you finished at least one guided practice. Missing today doesn't break it until the day is over. Every 7 days in a row gives bonus Moksha Points, and practising morning (or morning and night) for 21 days in a row completes a goal.

## Moksha Points: how they work
Moksha Points are the app's way of counting your practice and participation. You earn them by: opening the app each day; finishing guided practices (each once a day), with a bonus for all four in a day and for every 7-day streak; liking, commenting, asking questions and answering in the community (with daily limits); filling in each part of your profile, with a bonus at 100%; asking Ask Swamiji AI your first question of the day; completing goals; and gifts from Swamiji or admins. Exact amounts are shown in the app under Moksha Points, How it works.

## Moksha Points: giving points to another member
From Level 2, a member can send some of their Moksha Points to another member as a gift. The sender's balance goes down and the receiver's goes up by the same amount. Points received from other members can be spent or given again, but they don't raise the receiver's Level: Level only counts points you earned yourself. There is a limit per gift and per day. Swamiji and admins can add, remove or correct points, undo a gift, and pause sending for an account.

## Levels
Your Level grows with active days and earned Moksha Points. Level 2 needs 7 active days and 7 points; Level 3 needs 90 active days and 200 points; Level 4 needs 365 active days and 10,000 points. Membership (paid) has its own Member Level that grows with paid days. Higher levels open more of the app, such as likes from Level 2 and the daily morning and night practices at Level 4 (or with membership).

## Talking to Swamiji personally
Ask Swamiji AI is a guide based on Swamiji's recorded teachings, not Swamiji himself. For personal guidance, members can message or call Swamiji from the Ask tab (chat and calls are part of membership), or schedule a call. Swamiji may assign a senior member to help.

## Health and safety
Meditation and self-healing practices support well-being alongside medical care; they don't replace a doctor. Members with illness should keep following their doctor's advice. Anyone in crisis or thinking about harming themselves should reach a trusted person or emergency help immediately; in India, Tele-MANAS is free on 14416.
